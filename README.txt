QUERLIS AI WEBSITE STARTER
==========================

Contents
- index.html: homepage
- styles.css: responsive design
- script.js: mobile menu and demo form behaviour
- about.html, contact.html, privacy.html, disclaimer.html
- articles/: seven starter educational articles

PREVIEW ON YOUR PHONE OR COMPUTER
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. For publishing, upload the extracted files to a static hosting provider that supports custom websites.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH
- This is a static website starter, not yet a live website.
- Replace the placeholder Contact page with a real public business email or working contact form.
- The newsletter form is a demo only; it does not collect or send emails. Connect an email service before using it.
- Review and customize Privacy Policy and Disclaimer for the services actually used.
- Add a favicon, social sharing image, canonical domain, sitemap.xml and robots.txt after choosing a domain.
- Review every article, add your own experience and current verified references, and continue publishing original content. These starter articles alone do not guarantee Google AdSense approval.
- Before applying to AdSense, check Google's current official eligibility and publisher policies.
- Do not buy traffic, click your own ads, or promise visitors guaranteed income.
