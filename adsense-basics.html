WEBSITE MONETIZATION

Google AdSense: the basics for a new website

AdSense can display ads on eligible websites, but approval and income are not guaranteed. A useful website and real audience should come before ad placement.

Build a useful site first

Publish original, genuinely helpful content that serves a clear audience. Avoid copied articles, thin pages made only for ads, misleading claims, and artificial traffic.

Make the website complete

Use clear navigation, a mobile-friendly layout, working links, and relevant About, Contact, and Privacy pages. Review Google's current publisher policies and eligibility requirements before applying.

Understand what affects revenue

Revenue can vary with visitor location, topic, advertiser demand, page experience, ad formats, and the number of eligible visits. Traffic does not translate into a fixed guaranteed amount per view.

Important before applying

  • Use content you own or have permission to publish.
  • Do not click your own ads or ask others to click them.
  • Do not buy fake traffic or use bots.
  • Check current official AdSense rules and any country-specific requirements.
  • Do not treat sample earning estimates as promises.

This page is educational information, not a guarantee of acceptance or earnings.
