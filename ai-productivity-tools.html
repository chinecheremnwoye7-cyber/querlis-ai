AI TOOLS · PRODUCTIVITY

Use AI to make everyday work easier

A productivity tool is most useful when it reduces a specific bottleneck, rather than adding another app to manage.

Good starting tasks

  • Turn a long to-do list into a prioritised plan.
  • Brainstorm questions to ask before starting a project.
  • Convert rough notes into an outline you can edit.
  • Draft reusable checklists for recurring tasks.
  • Summarise non-sensitive information for faster review.

Keep control of important decisions

Do not upload confidential client, financial, medical, or personal information unless you understand the tool's privacy terms and have permission. Verify important output and keep a human review step for decisions that matter.

Measure whether it helps

Try one tool on one recurring task for a week. Compare the time taken, quality of the result, and effort needed to correct errors. Keep it only if it genuinely helps.
