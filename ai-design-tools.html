How to use AI design tools thoughtfully

AI-assisted design can help you explore concepts quickly, but good results still depend on a clear brief, careful editing, and respect for usage rights.

Start with a clear brief

Describe the audience, purpose, format, mood, and any required text. For example, a social post for a small bakery needs a different layout from a presentation cover.

Review before publishing

  • Check text, spelling, hands, faces, and other visual details.
  • Confirm image dimensions and readability on a phone.
  • Review the tool's commercial-use terms and any attribution requirements.
  • Avoid copying a specific artist's work or using logos and images without permission.

Keep a consistent style

Choose a small set of colours, fonts, and spacing rules. Reusing these choices can make a collection of graphics look more professional.
