Affiliate marketing basics

Affiliate marketing is a referral model: you share a tracked link to a product or service and may earn a commission when a qualifying action happens.

How it works

  1. Join a legitimate affiliate programme and read its terms.
  2. Choose products that are relevant to your audience.
  3. Create honest content that explains benefits, limits, and alternatives.
  4. Disclose the affiliate relationship clearly near the link.
  5. Track clicks and qualifying actions using the programme's reporting tools.

What makes it difficult?

You generally need an audience that trusts your recommendations. Commission rates, approval rules, cookie windows, payment methods, and product availability vary by programme and can change.

Build trust first

Do not invent reviews or claim to have used a product if you have not. Explain who a product may suit, who it may not suit, and where readers can verify the current terms. Affiliate income is not guaranteed.
